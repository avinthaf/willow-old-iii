## Design direction
- **Design system:** blue-collar
- **Primary color:** #1B3A6B — Deep navy blue; conveys reliability, trust, and professionalism — the standard palette for established trade contractors in the Midwest
- **Secondary color:** #B5651D — Copper/rust tone; references the literal material of plumbing pipes and reinforces a blue-collar, craft-oriented identity
- **Accent color:** #F5F5F0 — Warm off-white; keeps the layout clean and readable without sterile hospital-white; pairs naturally with navy and copper
- **Tone:** Blue-collar and direct — no marketing fluff. Short declarative sentences. The brand voice is earned through age and reputation, not salesmanship. "We've been here since 1911" does more work than any slogan.

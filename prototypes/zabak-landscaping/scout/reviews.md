## Watch-outs (not for the site)
- Review volume is critically low for a business of this age and reputation. Zabak has operated primarily through referrals with no online presence, so the digital footprint does not reflect actual client satisfaction. The developer should not overclaim review counts (do not write "hundreds of five-star reviews") — keep claims accurate and tied to verifiable platforms.
- The BBB lists two different phone numbers ((216) 398-9790 and (216) 214-0250) and the address appears under both "Cleveland, OH 44131" and "Independence, OH 44131" across different directories. Confirm the canonical contact details with Scott before publishing the site.
- The Angi 5.0 rating should be verified directly with Scott — the review count behind it is unclear from public data and may be a very small sample.

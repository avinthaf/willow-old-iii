## Design direction
- **Design system:** `fresh-outdoor`
- **Primary color:** #2D5016 — Deep foliage green; anchors the brand in nature and craft, evokes a well-maintained lawn, and signals experience
- **Secondary color:** #8B6914 — Warm earth/soil brown; grounds the palette in the physical work of landscaping, pairs naturally with green
- **Accent color:** #F4F0E6 — Off-white/cream; keeps the design clean and approachable, works as background for text-heavy sections without feeling cold
- **Tone:** Hardworking and humble — this is a craftsman who built a 35-year business on reputation, not advertising. Copy should be direct, grounded, and quietly confident. Avoid corporate language. Think: a neighbor you trust, not a franchise you Google.

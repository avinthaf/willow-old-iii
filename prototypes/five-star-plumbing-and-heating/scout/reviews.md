## Common themes

- **Kamran by name:** Nearly every review references the owner personally — customers are buying trust in him as an individual, not a faceless company
- **Price transparency and fairness:** Multiple reviewers contrast Kamran's quotes favorably against other estimates; the phrase "half of what other estimates came in at" appears unprompted
- **Same-day availability:** Several reviews mention same-day or next-day scheduling as a positive surprise
- **Punctuality:** "On time" is mentioned in nearly every detailed review
- **Education and communication:** Reviewers appreciate that Kamran explains what he is doing, what was wrong, and how to prevent future issues — not just fixing and leaving
- **Military discount:** At least one reviewer called this out as an appreciated gesture
- **Problem-solving under pressure:** One reviewer noted Kamran completed the job "at the promised price despite significantly more work than anticipated" — a strong integrity signal

## Watch-outs (not for the site)

- **Weekend availability:** Hours are Monday–Friday 8 AM–5 PM only. The site should not imply 24/7 or weekend emergency service unless that changes — doing so would generate calls Kamran cannot take and negative reviews from customers who expect round-the-clock access
- **Payment methods:** Currently cash and check only. No evidence of card or online payment. The prototype should not include a Stripe payment flow or imply online payment until the owner confirms this is changing
- **Solo or very small operation:** Reviews consistently refer to "he" — this appears to be Kamran working alone or with minimal staff. The site copy should not imply a large crew or fleet of trucks; that risks a mismatch between expectation and experience
- **Copyright date:** The current site shows a 2014 copyright, creating an impression of abandonment. The new site must have a current or auto-updating year in the footer

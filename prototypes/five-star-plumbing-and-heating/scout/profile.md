## Design direction
- **Design system:** blue-collar
- **Primary color:** #1A3A5C — Deep navy blue; conveys trust, reliability, and professionalism; matches the "five star" premium positioning without feeling corporate
- **Secondary color:** #F5A623 — Warm amber/gold; references the five-star rating theme visually, creates warmth and differentiates from generic blue-only trade sites
- **Accent color:** #FFFFFF — Clean white for text backgrounds and card surfaces; keeps the layout readable and modern
- **Tone:** Direct and trust-building — "blue-collar honest." No marketing fluff. Short sentences, real customer names, specific credentials, and a price-transparency message. The owner's name (Kamran) appears throughout because reviews show customers associate quality with him personally.
